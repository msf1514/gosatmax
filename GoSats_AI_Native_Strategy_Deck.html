Product and user understanding

Users arrive for rewards. They stay when those rewards feel trustworthy and useful.

What GoSats already does

Elite Card, bill pay, rent, brand vouchers, Gold and Sats wallets, reward history and investment products.

What the user expects

“What did I earn?” “Where can I earn more?” “Is this applicable to me?” “What is my easiest next step?”

Where value breaks

Generic offers, unclear calculations, zero-value first screens and recommendations disconnected from actual activity.

User groupsGold saver · Bitcoin accumulator · cashback optimizer · bill payer
Shared needMake everyday spending visibly build something valuable.
Design implicationLead with outcome, eligibility and next action—not product inventory.

What failed—and what it taught us

The agent failed at the journey moment before it failed at the model.

≈10

users in roughly three months for the chat-driven shopping agent.

A technically capable agent cannot create value when the user has no reason to invoke it.

1

Wrong moment

Users open a rewards app to check value or complete a payment—not plan a new purchase through chat.

2

Cold start

Too little user history meant retrieval could not produce meaningfully personal answers.

3

Trust asymmetry

Recommending where to spend requires more trust than showing how to reward a spend already happening.

4

Isolated experience

The agent sat beside the product instead of improving Home, Brands, Rewards and History.

Source: Internal agent performance and product post-mortem

Our approach

AI should act on existing signals or explain them back to the user.

01 · Embedded

Place intelligence inside the journeys users already visit. No separate “AI product” to discover.

02 · Proactive

Surface the best opportunity before the user asks, then make the next step one tap away.

03 · Explainable

Show the amount, route, eligibility and estimate before asking the user to act.

WhyTrust must precede recommendation.
User expectation“Help me without making me work for the answer.”
Product outcomeFaster first value and more repeat eligible payments.

What must be true

The design is viable only when data, consent and reward eligibility line up.

Data access

GoSats Card, registered BBPS bills, voucher history and consented Account Aggregator transactions.

→

Interpretation

Recurrence detection, narration classification, confidence scoring and user confirmation when uncertain.

→

Eligible action

Verified biller, voucher or card route with an approved rate shown before payment.

Important assumptionAA data does not provide a clean “Netflix” field.

Merchant identification depends on raw narration classification.

Required approvalsLivQuik · BBPS/billers · voucher partners · FIU/TSP · consent manager

Plus privacy, retention and model-monitoring approval.

Validation and execution

Validate trust and action before scaling intelligence.

1 · Prove value

First-ten-second comprehension, onboarding preference completion, first eligible transaction and recommendation click-through.

2 · Prove feasibility

First-party fields, BBPS coverage, eligible-route APIs, narration confidence and Account Aggregator partnership.

3 · Prove trust

Reward understanding, estimate accuracy, dismissal reasons, repeat route usage and Max answer resolution.

Phase 1Own data + registered bills + deterministic routes.
Phase 2Classification, ranking and grounded explanations.
Decision requiredLaunch cohort, approved routes, data tier and pilot success metric.

The goal is not to make GoSats look intelligent. It is to make every eligible payment more understandable and more rewarding.
