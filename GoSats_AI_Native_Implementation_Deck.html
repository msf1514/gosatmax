System context in one view

The architecture is a chain of trusted decisions, not a choice between API and MCP.

1 · Customer and app

The user chooses and confirms

  • Selects preferences
  • Reviews estimates
  • Chooses a route
  • Confirms payment or voucher action
→
2 · GoSats platform

GoSats decides what to show

  • Builds the Home and Get More response
  • Finds and ranks relevant opportunities
  • Checks reward rules and calculates estimates
  • Records confirmed rewards and explains them
↔
3 · Regulated and payment rails

Partners provide what GoSats cannot

  • LivQuik: card, wallet and KYC
  • Eligible FIU + TSP: consented external data
  • BBPS and voucher partners: payment routes
1 · Acquire permitted context

Account state, preferences and activity GoSats is allowed to use.

2 · Understand and prepare

Normalize activity, find patterns and assemble a candidate opportunity.

3 · Verify the value

Check a live route and calculate the reward with fixed rules.

4 · Execute and learn

The partner confirms completion; GoSats records reward and feedback.

Decision ownershipAI can help GoSats identify and explain an opportunity. GoSats rules own the value, and the partner rail owns completion.

For external activity, a bank or other FIP supplies the data, an Account Aggregator manages consent and transfer, an eligible FIU requests it, and a TSP can implement the connection. A TSP does not create FIU eligibility.

What must exist behind the redesign

Ten responsibilities become four understandable product layers.

This is the implementation map: each layer has a clear job, a source of truth and a safe result when the next dependency is unavailable.

Layer 1

Know the account and permission

Identity and eligibility: account, wallet and KYC state.

Preference capture: category, spend range and reward choice.

Permitted activity: GoSats data first; external data only with the right consent and regulated structure.

Produces: the correct journey and permitted context.
Layer 2

Understand existing behaviour

Transaction understanding: normalize descriptions and remove duplicates.

Pattern detection: identify repeat amount and timing.

Confidence and correction: stay broad when the merchant is uncertain.

Produces: a candidate behaviour, never a reward claim.
Layer 3

Verify that value is real

Route catalogue: billers, vouchers, rates, caps, KYC rules and availability.

Eligibility and reward calculation: one fixed calculation shared by every screen.

Freshness: every estimate carries an expiry.

Produces: a verified route and checkable estimate.
Layer 4

Deliver, confirm and improve

Opportunity, plan and ranking: decide what deserves attention.

Execution and ledger: partner confirms; GoSats records the reward.

Max: explains approved facts and hands actions back to controlled flows.

Produces: an action, confirmed outcome and feedback.
Where technology choices fitAPIs, events, callbacks and MCP connect these responsibilities. They do not replace them.

AI interprets uncertain information, ranks useful options and explains results. Fixed GoSats rules calculate money. Regulated and payment partners confirm external facts and completion.

What was redesigned

From permitted activity to a credible pattern

GoSats can detect a repeat payment without pretending it knows more than the evidence proves.

1
1
User value

A payment the user already makes becomes visible as a possible missed-reward opportunity.

2
Where the information comes from

Start with GoSats payments, registered bills and permitted GoSats card activity. External bank activity is an extension, not a launch dependency.

3
How the pattern is produced

Normalize the description, compare amount and timing, remove duplicates and assign confidence. Merchant rules run first; AI may classify only descriptions that remain unclear.

4
Safe wording and fallback

If merchant evidence is weak, say “a ₹499 payment appears to repeat monthly”, not “Netflix”. If confidence is low, ask the user or show nothing.

External-data gateFIP → Account Aggregator consent → eligible FIU → GoSats use. A TSP can implement the connection, but cannot grant FIU eligibility.

Until the legal purpose, eligible structure, retention controls and consent journey are resolved, the capability stays on GoSats-owned data and explicit user inputs.

08 / 15
A pattern is not yet an opportunity

GoSats must first prove that a live route exists and calculate the value with one fixed set of rules.

1
1
What the route catalogue holds

Supported billers and vouchers, reward currency, current rate, caps, exclusions, KYC requirement, partner availability, expiry and destination journey.

2
How value is calculated

A fixed reward capability receives the route and eligible amount, applies the current rules and returns Gold or sats, rupee worth, assumptions and expiry.

3
Why one source matters

Home, Get More, Brands, monthly plans and Max must all read the same estimate. The model never performs the financial calculation.

4
What happens when reality changes

An expired estimate is recalculated. An unavailable route is removed. If no verified route exists, the detected pattern is not presented as an earning opportunity.

Decision chain
Candidate behaviour → catalogue match → eligibility check
→ fixed reward calculation → estimate + assumptions + expiry

What deserves a place on Home

The opportunity capability decides whether the verified route is useful enough to interrupt the user.

1
1
Inputs to the decision

Detected behaviour, verified route, calculated value, confidence, freshness, previous dismissals and confirmed reward history.

2
What is stored

A short-lived opportunity containing the reason, wording level, route, estimate, last check, expiry and destination.

3
How placement works

Rank by expected user value and relevance. Show the strongest valid opportunity on Home; keep the wider list in Get More.

4
When it is suppressed or replaced

Hide low-value, low-confidence, dismissed, stale or unavailable options. “Show another way” fetches the next verified category rather than rewriting the same claim.

Important separationDetection finds behaviour. Verification proves value. Opportunity logic decides attention.

This prevents a model from turning an interesting pattern into an unsupported financial claim.

Get More becomes the permanent destination for bills, rent, card spends, vouchers, personalized opportunities and the monthly plan. Max remains a distinct support capability.

A calculation service creates the plan; Max explains it

The planner rearranges normal spending across valid GoSats routes. It does not recommend new spending.

12
1
What was redesigned

The small Max strip opens an itemized monthly plan showing total spend, expected Gold or Bitcoin and the routes that create it.

2
What problem it solves

Individual rewards may feel small. The plan shows the combined value of redirecting spending the user already expects to make.

3
How it is implemented

GoSats takes the selected categories, spend range and reward choice, removes unavailable routes, applies each route's limits and distributes the stated spend without counting a payment twice.

4
How it becomes actionable

Every row carries its assumptions and expiry. Tapping a row rechecks the value and opens that payment route; Best Route starts with the strongest still-valid option.

The plan is a calculation, not a model prediction Max can explain why a route appears, but fixed GoSats rules produce every amount. The plan never recommends spending more than the range provided by the user.

Personal ordering starts simple and improves with evidence

“Picked for your activity” reorders real offers. It never invents a cashback rate.

1
1
What was redesigned“Max picked for you” moves the most relevant real offers to the top while ordinary category browsing stays available below.
2
Why it was addedA long catalogue makes users search. A short personalized section reduces that effort without hiding the rest of the marketplace.
3
How it is implemented firstUse a transparent ordering rule based on selected categories, current reward value, recent GoSats activity and routes that are still available.
4
How it learns safelyRecord what was shown, opened, used, dismissed or corrected, and whether a reward was later confirmed. Use broad categories, never raw bank narration.
Fallback behaviour: if personalized ordering cannot be produced, the normal category sections remain. Do not add irrelevant cards merely to fill four spaces.
When to add machine learningOnly after the simpler rule has enough real outcome data.

A model must improve confirmed reward use without increasing dismissals.

Dependencies and how each one is resolved

The AI layer is shippable only after four product gates have clear owners and fallbacks.

1

Account state is reliable

Dependency: LivQuik account, wallet and KYC status.

Why: this decides the onboarding path, limits and whether KYC prompts appear.

ResolveMap every partner state to one GoSats screen. Keep PAN, address and OTP outside AI. If delayed, show the ordinary completion route.
2

Activity use is permitted

Dependency: lawful access and purpose-limited consent.

Why: recurring-payment intelligence cannot inspect external activity by assumption.

ResolveLaunch with owned data. For external data, confirm an eligible FIU structure, then connect through AA using an internal FIU module or TSP.
3

The route can complete

Dependency: BBPS, voucher, issuer, card and reward-ledger partners.

Why: a recommendation is useless if its payment path is unavailable.

ResolveRefresh availability before showing value, recheck on tap and wait for partner confirmation. Otherwise hide the route or offer a verified alternative.
4

Max stays inside approved facts

Dependency: model provider, approved knowledge, safe tools and evaluation.

Why: conversation cannot become a second source of financial truth.

ResolveRedact sensitive data, permit read-only facts, log tool use and test answers. Fall back to approved help or a controlled app journey.
Technology follows responsibilityUse the simplest connection that preserves ownership, auditability and fallback.

APIs connect product capabilities, events trigger background work, callbacks confirm partner outcomes, and MCP may standardize assistant tools later. None replaces GoSats rules, consent or partner confirmation.

The delivery sequence

Build the trustworthy reward engine first. Add intelligence only where it improves the decision.

1 · Connect the visible flows

Account state, preferences, route catalogue, reward rules and every click-through.

2 · Prove value with owned data

Repeat-pattern checks, verified opportunities, feedback and monthly plans using activity GoSats can already use.

3 · Clear the external-data gate

Confirm FIU eligibility or regulated structure, choose in-house build or TSP, then implement Account Aggregator consent and controls.

4 · Add Max safely

Approved knowledge, read-only tools, redaction, answer tests, monitoring and safe fallbacks.

The build is complete when

Every button worksReal next screen or a clear unavailable state.
Every amount can be checkedKnown route, calculation rule and reward record.
Every suggestion has a reasonSource, last check and clear explanation.
The user stays in controlDismiss, correct, replace and revoke.
Core flows survive failurePayments and rewards do not depend on AI.
Value is measurableMore confirmed reward use, not only more clicks.
Operating model

The GoSats backend runs the experience. Max reads approved facts. Partners complete regulated actions.

AI recognizes, orders and explains. GoSats checks the route and calculates the estimate. The partner confirms completion. The reward record proves the outcome.
